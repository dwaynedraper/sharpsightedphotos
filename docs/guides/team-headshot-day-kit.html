Step one

Decide five things.

Get these settled first. Everything else falls into place behind them.

  • Headcount. Include remote staff and anyone starting in the next 60 days. Shooting them now is cheaper than a second trip later.
  • The date. Wednesdays and Thursdays go first. Mondays lose people to catch-up, and Fridays lose them to time off.
  • Who gets the Featured Set. Founders, partners, and anyone whose face is part of the brand. Everyone else gets the Standard Set.
  • The room. One room or a quiet corner for most of the day. Page 6 has the checklist.
  • A cutoff for anyone who'll miss it. Pick the date they need to tell you by. They can join a new hire reshoot later.

Step two

The email to your team.

Paste this, fill in the blanks, and hit send.

Subject: Headshots on date. Pick your time.

Hi team,

We're updating everyone's headshots on date so our website and LinkedIn finally match. Dean Draper from Sharp Sighted Photos is setting up a small studio in room.

It takes about 10 to 15 minutes. If you're getting the Featured Set, plan on 30.

Pick your time here. link to the sign-up sheet

A few tips for looking your best.

  • Wear solid colors. Skip big logos and busy patterns.
  • Dress like you're meeting an important client.
  • Not sure about a top? Bring a second one. We'll pick on the monitor.
  • Glasses are fine. Wear them if you usually do.
  • Getting the Featured Set? Bring a second outfit and the tools of your work.

Not a fan of having your photo taken? You're in good hands. Dean directs the whole time, and you'll see every frame on a monitor as you go.

Step four

The room check.

Walk the room with this list. If most of the boxes get a check, we're in great shape.

  • About 10 by 12 feet of clear floor. A conference room with the table pushed aside works.
  • A ceiling 8 feet or higher. Most offices clear this easily.
  • Two outlets nearby. Standard wall outlets are all I need.
  • A small table. This is where the review monitor sits.
  • A mirror close by. A restroom down the hall counts.
  • A door that closes, or a quiet corner. People relax faster when the whole office isn't walking by.
  • Parking near a door. I bring a few cases of gear in and out.

Step five

The day, start to finish.

  • 01
    I arrive and set up. About an hour before the first slot. You don't need to babysit me.
  • 02
    The first person sits. Everyone sees their frames on a 15-inch monitor and picks favorites right there.
  • 03
    Breaks happen where the sheet says. If someone runs late, I shuffle the order. Nobody gets skipped.
  • 04
    Last slot, then I pack out. The room is back to normal in under an hour.
  • 05
    Edited images arrive in 3 to 5 business days. Need them sooner? A 48-hour rush is $150.
